FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the apartment reservation workflow work? +
Browse through our active apartment complexes or individual suite units, select your desired unit, and click "Reserve Suite". You will configure your down payment ratio (10%, 20%, or 30%) and installment term. Once submitted with proof of transfer, our sales officers verify and lock the suite.
What payment methods are supported for the down payment? +
We accept bank wire transfers, inward investment accounts (IIA) for foreign or non-resident Sri Lankan buyers, manager's cheques, certified deposit slips, and direct online banking facilities verified by our Finance & Payments team.
Can foreign nationals or non-resident Sri Lankans purchase units? +
Yes. Non-resident Sri Lankans and international investors can legally acquire condominium units in Sri Lanka above the specified regulatory floors under existing property regulations. Full legal assistance is provided.
How can external agents list properties on Living-Ora? +
Registered external agents and verified property owners can list resale apartments through our Resale Listings page with complete specifications, deed validation, and pricing details.
